Objectives

Overall aim

To treat the human elbow, its muscles, bones and joint, as a deformable solid system. We start from how a muscle is built and how it produces force, then apply the tools of Mechanics of Solids (equilibrium, stress and strain, traction and Mohr's circle, beam theory) to find the forces and stresses in the arm during a curl. Finally we test those results against how the real body works.
