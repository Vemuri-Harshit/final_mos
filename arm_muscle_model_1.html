Model and equations (click to open)

Static, planar, rigid rods, upper arm fixed and vertical, muscle is a straight line from the shoulder to the forearm. Elbow is the origin O, the shoulder is at height Lu, the muscle attaches at distance a along the forearm, the load acts at distance Lf.

L = √(Lu² + a² − 2·Lu·a·cosθ) muscle length (law of cosines)

d = Lu·a·sinθ / L moment arm of the muscle about the elbow

τ = (W·Lf + Wf·Lf/2)·sinθ torque of the load about the elbow (Wf is the optional forearm weight)

F = τ / d = (W·Lf + Wf·Lf/2)·L / (Lu·a) sinθ cancels, so F is proportional to the muscle length

β is the angle between the muscle line and the forearm; the force acts along the muscle, from the insertion toward the shoulder.

R = −F·(unit vector along muscle) − (0, −(W + Wf)) joint reaction, from force balance on the forearm

Edit the constants in CFG at the top of the script to change the arm. Illustrative values, not a medical or training tool.
